How do you measure?  
But remember a measure tells little of those it measures  
And instead tells volumes of the man measuring.

Many speak of their measures  
A number in a bank account  
A number of followers  
A number of drinks  
A number of degrees  
A number of countries  
To them I wish safe travels

Many hide their measures  
Cause sometimes it is not safe  
But often these people hold great treasures

I will tell you my measure  
Cause I am proud, after all  
I spent many years crafting my measure

I measure a man by his care  
And by how many people would say he was there
