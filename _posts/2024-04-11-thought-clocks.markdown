Tick tick  
I see them everywhere  
For the clerk it's 30 seconds  
For my father its 30 years  
For my college friends its 2 months

Sometimes we add time  
But most of the time we don't  
Sometimes we promise to add time  
But most of the time we don't

Most clocks never change  
The few that get extra time are lucky  
But most clocks are about right  
They ring when it's time to move

I hate the clocks  
But I need them  
Otherwise I forget  
What time it is
