I’m past a quarter  
But this game only got three  
To my self I am closer  
To my self I am almost free

I compare less, but I still do  
I reminisce more, oh yes I do  
I slow down, cause I don’t wanna miss out  
I speed up, cause I don’t wanna miss out

How can I keep up with it all?  
Well I don’t I say.  
I keep up with a few.  
My mates of today.

But many will become mates of yesterday  
Cause most come for a visit but not for a stay  
And with that I am okay  
Thats the age speaking I would say

Life has been recently good  
But in the corner the reaper stares with his hood  
So I will enjoy this mood  
Hope it stays, knock on wood

I have now studied in the schools of Boult and Brown  
And will soon have cap and gown  
Some will still call me a clown  
To them I won’t bow down

I am slowing down to smell a tree  
But perhaps I may not become faculty  
Thats ok with my friends I’ll have tea  
They are the ones who show loyalty

Its now five past twenty  
Maybe I’ll make a century
