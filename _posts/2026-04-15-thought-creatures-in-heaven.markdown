Inspired by Glass Animals

Two in the morning, leaving the bus  
No one around, but the two of us  
Stroll down the street dancing to our songs  
A crazy dance for which my heart longs

It tears through my head, does it haunt you too

Dancing in the dark, the city of dream  
We held each other like we were only two  
Held me so close, just me and you

It tears through my head, does it haunt you too  
I never really said that I loved you too  
Lucky, lucky, you, cause I’m fortune's fool  
Such small words but they hit so huge

I don’t think I realize  
Just how much I miss you sometimes  
We were young and so in love  
We were just creatures in heaven

Three in the morning, safe inside  
Bury me here in your warm style  
Three in the morning, be in the moment  
Here in the moment, free in the moment

Five in the evening, over like that  
Go slow-motion, cut it to black  
Cause its merely a moment, here for a moment  
Here is the moment, here for a moment

It tears through my head does it haunt you too  
I never really said that i loved you too  
Lucky, lucky, you cause I was an idiot, a fool  
Such small words I have yet to use
