They are today with me  
My friends many from across the sea  
So I shall enjoy today  
Because tomorrow we may part

We will still be friends  
But we no longer talk  
We call for birthdays  
But we grow apart

We will meet and say things haven't changed  
But we know we are playing a part  
You're a father and I an uncle  
Our college days are long gone

I wish back then I knew what today meant  
That today could be the last today  
Oh I would have told you  
I love you
