I'm a curious researcher in computational photography. My current work focuses on generative camera editing and camera pipelines. Previously, I have worked on auto-exposure, image restoration, and color correction.

I am a PhD graduate of York University, supervised by [Michael Brown](https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/~mbrown/), and a visiting student at the University of Toronto under [David Lindell](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~lindell/) and [Kyros Kutulakos](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kyros/). I previously was an undergraduate research student under [Terrance Boult](https://vast.uccs.edu/~tboult/).

I most recently interned at Sony AI Japan with [Daisuke Iso](https://ai.sony/people/daisuke-iso) and [Feiran Li](https://www.linkedin.com/in/feiran-li-475503170/). I have also interned at Samsung AI Center Toronto twice, mentored by [Michael Brown](https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/~mbrown/), [Mahmoud Afifi](https://www.mafifi.info/), [Junyong Lee](https://junyonglee.me/), and [Abhijith Punnappurath](https://abhijithpunnappurath.github.io/). Previously, I interned at Adobe on [Marc Levoy](https://graphics.stanford.edu/~levoy/)'s NextCam team, mentored by [Shumian Xin](https://shumianxin.github.io/), [Zhoutong Zhang](https://ztzhang.info/), and [Cecilia Zhang](https://ceciliavision.github.io/).
